Why keep records now

Since 2024, platforms like Vinted, eBay and Etsy send HMRC details of sellers who sell 30 or more items, or earn about £1,700 (€2,000) or more, in a year (gov.uk). Being reported doesn't mean you owe tax: the rules haven't changed. But if you're trading and your sales are over £1,000 a year, you need to register for Self Assessment (trading allowance). Then you'll want your income and costs in one place before the 31 January deadline.

Only selling your own old things? HMRC says that's usually not trading, so normally there's no tax to pay (Help for Hustles). You probably don't need this sheet. It's for people who buy to resell or make things to sell. Not sure which you are? Our free guides: Do I pay tax on eBay sales? and Do I pay tax on Vinted?

Honest notes

  • It's a record-keeping template, not tax advice. It adds up your figures; it doesn't decide whether you're trading or what you owe. For that, check gov.uk or a qualified accountant.
  • Tax figures were checked against gov.uk in July 2026. Platform fees are a snapshot, and each shows its last-checked date.
  • eBay business fees use the general-category rate (11.9%), and Etsy fees leave out Offsite Ads. The free calculator handles both exactly.
  • Months run 1 April to 31 March. HMRC accepts accounts made up to 31 March without splitting off the days to 5 April (HS222).
  • Built with AI (Claude) and tested automatically: the workbook is recalculated and checked against 23 tests, including the fee examples above worked by hand.

Questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets (File → Import also works). It also opens in Excel, LibreOffice Calc and Apple Numbers.

Which tax year is it for?

You pick: 2025/26 (6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026, whose online return is due 31 January 2027) or 2026/27. The totals only count sales dated in the year you choose.

I sell on a platform that isn't listed.

Choose "Other" and type the platform's fee into the Other costs column. Totals still work.

Do you see my figures?

No. It's a file on your computer or in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to us.
