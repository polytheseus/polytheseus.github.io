Free tools that do one paperwork job well

Checkers, planners, builders and calculators for teachers, trades, bookkeepers, estate agents and online sellers. Each one sits on a guide that explains the rules behind it.

Your data stays with you. These tools run as JavaScript in your own browser. Nothing you paste or type is sent to us or stored, so you can use real class lists, bank lines and listings. The tax calculators give estimates for education, not tax advice.

For teachers

Rubric checker

Paste a rubric from ChatGPT, Google Docs, Sheets or Word. It flags judgement words like "good" with nothing to see, levels that differ only by "some" and "most", rows that mark neatness or effort instead of learning, and too many or too few levels.

Guide: how to make a rubric with ChatGPT

Parents' evening notes builder

One line per student (initials, strength, next step, home step) becomes a timed slot list, a 5-part script per student, a follow-up message and a printable sheet. It flags "try harder"-style targets, praise with no example, full names and sensitive details.

Guide: parents' evening script and notes template

Math comment builder

Pick a grade band (K–2, 3–5 or 6–8) and a level for one to three strands (times tables, place value, fractions, word problems, explaining thinking, geometry, data, equations…) and it writes a math report card comment with a next step and an everyday home tip, in US or UK (maths) wording. Paste any comment to check it for "careless", "not a math person", sets, jargon and diagnosis words.

Guide: report card comments for math

Report card writing planner

Enter your class size, comments per child, minutes per comment, the days you can work and the deadline, and it gives you a day-by-day plan ("Mon 9 Nov: children 1–3, about 24 min") with proofreading days kept free. It tells you if the plan doesn't fit and what would, and adds it to your calendar.

Report card season kit: every comment builder in one place

Behavior and work habits comment builder

Pick a grade band and a level for one to three habits (listening, staying on task, organization, homework, effort, kindness, teamwork, managing feelings, routines…) and it writes a behavior report card comment with a next step and a home routine, in US, UK or Canadian (Ontario learning skills) wording. Paste any comment to check it for labels like "disruptive", incidents and sanctions, diagnosis words and jargon.

Guide: report card comments for behavior and work habits

Reading and writing comment builder

Pick a grade band (K–2, 3–5 or 6–8) and a level for one to three skills (phonics, reading aloud, comprehension, reading between the lines, spelling, sentences, paragraphs, ideas, editing, speaking…) and it writes an ELA report card comment with a next step and an everyday home tip, in US or UK wording. Paste any comment to check it for "reluctant reader", "poor speller", reading levels, jargon and diagnosis words.

Guide: report card comments for reading and writing

Comment builder for struggling students

Tick a strength and the area that's hardest right now (reading, math facts, focus, talking, missing work, confidence, attendance…), say how serious it is, and it writes an honest, kind report card comment: strength, the difficulty in plain words, the support in place, a home tip and an invitation to talk. Paste any comment to check it for labels like "lazy", "potential" lines, comparisons and diagnosis words.

Guide: report card comments for struggling students

Preschool comment builder

Pick a level for two or three areas (friends, feelings, talking, counting, climbing…) and it writes a pre-K or nursery progress report comment with a next step and a home tip, or a UK progress check at age 2 summary under the three prime areas. Paste any comment to check it for labels like "clingy", verdicts, comparisons, diagnosis words and jargon.

Guide: preschool progress report comments

Kindergarten comment builder

Pick a level (strength, on track, next step) for two or three skills and it writes a kindergarten or Reception report comment with a next step and a home tip. Paste any comment to check it for labels, "behind" and "can't", comparisons, diagnosis words, jargon, leftover placeholders and mixed pronouns.

Guide: kindergarten report card comments

Kahoot quiz checker

Paste a quiz from ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude and it checks Kahoot's import limits, answer letters, duplicates, "all of the above", giveaway answers and "the answer is always B". It spreads the answers across A–D and gives you rows for Kahoot's template, a CSV, a student copy and an answer key.

Guide: how to make a Kahoot quiz with ChatGPT

Sub plan builder

Class details plus one line per block (time, task, materials, what to collect) become a one-page sub plan or cover work sheet to print. It flags tasks that need your login, tech with no paper fallback, new content, vague tasks, nothing to collect, and student details that don't belong on the desk.

Guide: sub plan template and emergency sub plans

For trades

Invoice and receipt maker

Add a line for each thing you're charging for and it writes a UK invoice or receipt with the due date and how to pay, does VAT, the construction reverse charge wording and CIS deductions on labour only, and flags anything missing: supply date, your own name as a sole trader, company details, bank details.

UK · Guide: invoice template for trades

Quote maker and checker

Type your scope lines and prices and pick exclusions for your trade. It writes a full quote or estimate with payment terms, adds up the VAT, adds the homeowner cancellation wording when it's needed, and flags vague words like "etc." and "as required".

UK · Guide: how to write a quote for a job

Review reply writer and checker

Pick the kind of bad review (fair, untrue, 1-star with no words, not a customer, damage claim) and it writes a calm public reply, a private message and whether to report it. Paste any reply and it flags accusations, legal threats, review-for-refund offers, customers' addresses and non-apologies.

UK and US · Guide: how to reply to a bad Google review

Late payment interest calculator

Enter an unpaid invoice and the date it was due. It works out UK statutory interest (8% plus the right Bank Rate) and the £40, £70 or £100 compensation for business customers, or the 8% court-claim figure for homeowners, and writes out the sums to paste into a final reminder.

UK · Guide: customer won't pay

Client onboarding plan builder

Bookkeeping fee calculator

Work out your target hourly rate from the income you need and your real billable hours, turn a client's monthly workload into a fixed fee, quote catch-up work with written assumptions, and build a price list that downloads for Excel or Google Sheets.

UK and US · Guide: how much to charge for bookkeeping

Month-end close checklist builder

UK and US · Guide: month-end close checklist

Bank reconciliation checker

Enter the statement and book balances and paste the items on one side only. Get the reconciliation statement, any unexplained difference with its likely cause (one item, a reversed entry, swapped digits), stale-item warnings, and a download for Excel or Google Sheets.

UK and US · Guide: how to do a bank reconciliation

Bookkeeping cleanup plan builder

Describe a catch-up or clean-up job and get the statements to request for every account and month, the filing deadlines those months feed (with any already passed), the order to work in, a tailored checklist and the email to the client. Downloads for Excel or Google Sheets.

UK and US · Guide: bookkeeping cleanup checklist

Put a free tool on your own website

Run a blog, a trade association site, a school or training page? Any of these tools can sit on your page for free. Pick one, copy the code and paste it into your page (WordPress: a "Custom HTML" block). The tool runs in your visitors' browsers, so nothing they type is sent to you or to us, and it keeps itself up to date.
