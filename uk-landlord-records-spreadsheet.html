Why keep records this way now

From 6 April 2026 landlords and sole traders with more than £50,000 of qualifying income must keep digital records and send HMRC quarterly updates; from April 2027 it's more than £30,000 (tested on 2025/26 income), and from April 2028 more than £20,000 (gov.uk). Qualifying income is your gross rent (your share) plus any self-employment turnover, not your profit. Not sure if that's you? The free £30,000 test works it out.

Each update has to use HMRC's categories and cover the tax year so far (gov.uk, send quarterly updates). This sheet uses the exact names in HMRC's quarterly update direction, so the totals match what your bridging software asks for. Even if you're not in MTD yet, the same figures fill in the SA105 pages of your tax return.

Raising the rent this year? In England, since 1 May 2026, the only way is a Section 13 notice on Form 4A. Our free rent increase date calculator gives the earliest start date and the last day to serve.

Questions

Is a spreadsheet allowed for Making Tax Digital?

Yes, with bridging software. HMRC accepts spreadsheets as digital records if the figures reach HMRC digitally, for example through linked cells or a CSV import into bridging software; retyping or copying and pasting between sheets is not allowed. This sheet keeps every quarterly total in a fixed cell so bridging tools can read it. It does not send anything to HMRC itself.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets. It also opens in Excel, LibreOffice Calc and Apple Numbers.

How does it handle a jointly owned property?

You enter the full rent or bill and your share on the Setup tab (for example 50%). Every total then uses your share, which is what you report.

Where does mortgage interest go?

Pick 'Residential finance costs'. It goes into the quarterly update and SA105 box 44, but isn't taken off your profit: for residential lets HMRC gives a basic-rate tax reduction instead: 20% up to 2026/27, and 24% for 2027/28 (the new property basic rate announced at Budget 2026, not yet law). The sheet keeps it separate and estimates the reduction for the year you pick.

Is this tax advice?

No. It's a record-keeping template that adds up your figures in HMRC's categories. It doesn't work out your tax. Check gov.uk or an accountant for your situation.

Do you see my figures?

No. It's a file on your computer or in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to us.
