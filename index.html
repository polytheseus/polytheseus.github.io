How it works

  1. Pick your job's pack. It's a set of plain text files: the rules, steps and checklists an experienced person in your job follows.
  2. Add it to your AI app. Copy the instructions, upload one file. A one-minute video shows you where to click in ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude.
  3. Send rough notes, get a proper draft. It asks when something's missing, checks its own work, and never makes up facts or prices. You read it and send it.

Why not just ask the AI?

Plain prompt

"Mia is a pleasure to have in class and should keep trying hard."

With a pack

A specific strength, the evidence from your notes, and one next step a parent can help with. Behaviour described, not labelled. Checked before you see it.

AI assistants are good writers but don't know your job's unwritten rules: what a parent needs to hear, what a customer always assumes is included in a quote, what a seller actually nets. Each pack writes those rules down, so you don't have to explain them every time.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Each free pack is open source and yours to keep. Pro packs are a one-off payment with updates included, and if one doesn't work as described, tell us what went wrong within 14 days for a refund.

Which AI apps does it work with?

ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, including on your phone. Microsoft Copilot works with the copy-and-paste prompt pack. Coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Gemini CLI can install the packs as skills.

Do you see what I type?

No. A pack is text files that live inside your own AI app, so nothing comes to us. Your AI provider's own privacy terms apply, which is why the packs use initials or [Name] instead of real names wherever people are involved.

Can I trust what it writes?

Treat it like a capable new colleague's first draft: much better than a blank page, and still yours to check. The packs are built to flag what they're unsure of and never invent facts, prices or figures.

About Polytheseus

Polytheseus is a small experiment: can an AI agent build things useful enough that people pay for them? Claude, an AI agent made by Anthropic, does most of the work. It researches each job's rules, writes and tests the packs, and builds this site.

It's run by Robin Hale, who sets the direction, looks after accounts and support, and steps in wherever a human is needed. Sales pay for the agent's running costs.
